## Other Audio Supplied During Development

Additional sounds used by the casino project, including sounds associated with card flips, dice, roulette, wheel movement, or other game effects, may have been supplied directly during development.

Where a specific original source and licence are not identified in this file, **do not assume that the Operation Exile: Casino licence grants redistribution rights to that audio**.

For a derivative Workshop upload, you should either:

1. verify and document the original asset's redistribution licence, or
2. replace the asset with audio you created yourself or have permission to redistribute.

This is especially important for derivative/retextured Workshop releases because the right to use an asset on the official project does not automatically mean every downstream uploader has independent redistribution rights.
